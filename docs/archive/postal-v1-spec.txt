01. PRODUCT DEFINITION — FIXED
=====================================================================

Name:
DEAD LETTER

Subtitle:
The night post between worlds.

Primary hook:
Somebody mailed a lie to the afterlife. Find it.

Player role:
Night clerk at a small supernatural post office.

Core task:
Each letter contains four factual statements.
Exactly one is false.
Select the false statement and stamp RETURN TO SENDER.

Session:
Six letters.

Default answering time:
25 seconds of active answering time per letter.

Default mode:
Daily Shift.

Other modes:
Practice Shift.
Relaxed Practice.

Confidence mechanic:
Two wax seals per shift.
A seal doubles the points for one correct submitted answer.

Scoring:
Correct, no seal: 100.
Correct, seal: 200.
Incorrect: 0.
Unanswered timeout: 0.
Maximum score: 800.
No speed bonus.
No negative score.
No streak multiplier.

No:
Accounts.
Multiplayer.
Live AI.
Open-answer judging.
Crowd rarity estimates.
Paid items.
Ads.
Social feeds.
Online leaderboards.
Fake player counts.
Artificial loading delays.
Backend requirements.

Audience:
People who enjoy short daily knowledge games, attractive tactile interfaces,
interesting factual corrections, and spoiler-free result sharing.

Mood:
Cozy supernatural bureaucracy.
Inviting, witty, quiet, slightly uncanny.
Never gore, jump scares, punishment-heavy horror, or grim dystopia.

12. TIMER AND SEAL BEHAVIOR
=====================================================================

Timer display:
A small circular SVG track plus numeric seconds.
Text alternative states remaining seconds.
Do not announce every second.

25–11 seconds:
Quiet ink/teal.

10–6 seconds:
Brass accent.

5–1 seconds:
Vermilion accent and a visible “Time remaining” label.
No flashing.
No alarm sounds.
No shaking.

At 0:
Resolve exactly once as unanswered.
Show the reveal screen.
Do not submit a merely selected answer.

Timer starts:
Only after the letter has opened and claims are ready.

Timer stops:
Immediately on valid submit.
When any modal opens.
When document becomes hidden.
When navigation leaves active answering.
When Resume overlay is active.

After hiding or reloading:
Show Resume.
Do not restart automatically.

After closing ordinary Settings/About/Collection:
Resume active answering with the preserved remaining time.
No extra Resume dialog is necessary unless the app was hidden/reloaded.

Seal:
Clicking the seal control arms/disarms it.
Arming does not consume it.
Submit consumes it atomically.
An incorrect sealed answer still consumes it.
Timeout does not consume an armed but unsubmitted seal.
Clear armed state before the next round.
Cannot arm when none remain.
Tooltip/help text must not be the only rule explanation.
